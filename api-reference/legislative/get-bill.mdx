title: 'Get bill'

---

import BillFields from '/snippets/legislative/bill-fields.mdx';

Returns a single bill with its sponsors. To retrieve the bill's records, such as its actions or votes, use the endpoints under **Bill records**.

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="bill_id" type="string" required>
  The `id` of the bill, from [List bills](/api-reference/legislative/list-bills) or [Get legislative source](/api-reference/legislative/get-source).
</ParamField>

## Response

<BillFields />

<ResponseField name="sponsors" type="array">
  The bill's sponsors, in the order the legislature lists them.

  <Expandable title="Sponsor fields">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">
      UUID of the sponsor record.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string">
      Name of the sponsor.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="party" type="string">
      The sponsor's party, as a short key such as `"lab"`. Empty if unknown.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="member_from" type="string">
      The sponsor's constituency or other affiliation. Empty if unavailable.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="organisation" type="string">
      The government department or ministerial office the sponsor acts for. Empty if none.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sponsor_type" type="string">
      `"PRIMARY"` for the member in charge of the bill, or `"COSPONSOR"`. Empty if unknown.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="external_member_id" type="string">
      The sponsor's member ID in the legislature's own system. Empty if unavailable.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sort_order" type="integer">
      Position of the sponsor in the legislature's list.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `403 Forbidden` | Your account doesn't have a valid subscription or hasn't accepted the Terms of Service. |
| `404 Not Found` | No bill has this `id`, or the bill belongs to a jurisdiction you can't access. |
